You can customize Google Chrome to fit your chosen aesthetic. This guide provides the steps to install a new theme on your browser.

## Prerequisites

- A stable internet connection
- [An up-to-date installation of Google Chrome](https://google.com/chrome)

## Instructions

<Tabs>
<TabItem value="settings" label="Method 1: Chrome Settings" default>

1. Open **Google Chrome**.
2. In the top-right corner of the browser window, click the **More** options icon (the three vertical dots or kebab icon).

## Resetting to the default theme

To remove a custom theme and restore Chrome's original appearance:

1. Open **Settings** and select **Appearance**.
2. Next to **Theme**, click **Reset to default**.

:::info
On Linux, Google Chrome can adapt to your desktop environment's **GTK** or **Qt** settings. In the **Appearance** menu, you can toggle between your desktop theme (**Use GTK+**) and Chrome's default theme (**Use classic**).
:::
